# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person at their desk, usually in the evening with the room lights low, about to play on the PS5 through PS Remote Play in the win11 VM. The launcher opens on the second monitor (DP-2, LG UltraFine) next to the LG HDR 4K that will show the game. They look at it for ten seconds, pick how the VM should reach the LG, press Enter and pick up the controller.

## Product Purpose

Start the VM and set up its display path in one action: Looking Glass at 1080p upscaled with FSR 1, Looking Glass at native 4K with no filters, or the iGPU's HDMI output with the LG switched over. Then show what is live (VM, layout, LG input, Remote Play, virtual DS4, recording) and offer the ways back (to the host desktop, shut down).

## Brand Personality

Quiet instrument: calm, precise and hardware-like, the way a good AV switcher is. It explains the signal route instead of decorating it, and gets out of the way once the game is on.

## Anti-references

- RGB gamer launchers: neon glow, angled HUD panels, purple and cyan.
- Console branding (PlayStation blue, PS glyphs).
- SaaS dashboards: card grids and big-number tiles.

## Design Principles

1. Show the route. The signal-path diagram is the explanation; every option changes it visibly.
2. Impossible combinations are disabled with a reason, never silently ignored.
3. Motion only reports real progress: steps animate when the backend reports them, not on a timer.
4. Dark because the room is dark and a game is on the next screen; no bright surfaces.
5. Keyboard first: arrows and Enter do everything.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honour prefers-reduced-motion. Full keyboard operation with visible focus. Text contrast at least 4.5:1 on the graphite surfaces. State is never shown by colour alone (dots carry a word).
